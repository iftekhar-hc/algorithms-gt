AI Security Research Engineer

**Company:**

Qualcomm Technologies, Inc.

**Job Overview:**

Qualcomm’s System Security R&D and Standards group is seeking a candidate for the position of Artificial Intelligence Security Research Engineer. You will play a crucial role in advancing the company's mission to push the boundaries of Artificial Intelligence (AI) security research. The role involves supporting the team of Researchers by deploying tools and infrastructure and conducting cutting-edge experiments, to secure critical products in core AI domains in LLMs/VLMs/VLAs, foundation models, deep learning, computer vision, and multimodal research.

As a Research Engineer, you will be responsible for developing and rapidly iterating on innovative research ideas, collaborating with other researchers, engineers, or research residents to prototype them. Stay up to date with the latest developments in the field, you will contribute to top-tier AI and security conferences through publications. Your work will have real-world impact, with opportunities to collaborate across teams or business units globally to deploy your ideas on millions of devices, such as mobile phones, AR/VR headsets, and autonomous vehicles.

We are seeking a Research Engineer to join our team focused on pushing the boundaries of AI systems security, especially for resource-constrained environments. This role is ideal for candidates with a proven track record in building scalable, efficient, and cutting-edge AI systems, and a passion for making advanced secure AI accessible on edge devices.

The ideal candidate will be a highly self-directed individual with strong coding skills, and a passion for AI and cybersecurity. We are considering candidates with various levels of experience. We are flexible on location and open to hiring anywhere, with preferred locations in USA, or Europe.

**Key responsibilities**

- Support world-class research by implementing the ideas.
- Deploy and maintain the AI infrastructure and keep up to date with best practices.
- Develop experiments on different Qualcomm hardware.

**Requirements**

- Minimum requirement: Bachelor degree in Computer Science specialized in AI (e.g., deep learning, computer vision, or a related field) with AI/MLOps experience.
- Experience: A proven track record of achievements in building successful project demos, prototypes or products is a strong plus.
- Skills: Clear and effective communication. Ability to work effectively across disciplinary boundaries and collaborate with product development teams. Proven ability to implement and evaluate solutions. Proficient in Python with hands-on experience using deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow. Experience with Qualcomm development kits/platforms (e.g., QAIRT, Hexagon-MLIR, AIMET) is a must.

**Qualifications:**

Bachelor in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or related field (cybersecurity, privacy, artificial intelligence).

•    Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 2+ years of Systems Engineering or related work experience.
